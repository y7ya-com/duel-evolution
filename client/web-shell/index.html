Duel Evolution

Duel Evolution is a free online card-dueling game that runs in your browser. It revives Yu-Gi-Oh! Online: Duel Evolution, also known as Yu-Gi-Oh! Online 2 (2007–2012): the same world, the same duels, with nothing to download or install.

How to play

Open the page, sign in, and pick a starter deck. Walk through the world, step into a lobby, and challenge other players or the computer-controlled duelists you meet.

Features

  • Real-time duels against other players
  • Duels against NPC opponents
  • A deck editor to build and tune your decks
  • Free Duel mode for quick duels
  • Lobbies and chat with other duelists
  • Plays on desktop, tablet and phone, in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari

Duel Evolution is a fan revival. It is not official and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Konami.
